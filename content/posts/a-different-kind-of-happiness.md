If there's something I learn from my life, you genuinely only get lucky once. At least, on one aspect of your life. You pay the price of getting a new job by failing at the romance front. Perhaps this is the true meaning of _A Different Kind of Happiness._ You are happy, you were given happiness by the Universe, but you will _suffer elsewhere_ as it's only a fair trade that way. 

A year ago, I wish I was given the foresight so that _maybe_ I could influence my story into taking a different route. If only I knew just how bad things are gonna be, I would've been more present. I wouldn't have gone and chase my futile dreams. I would've said no to an opportunity and said yes to staying still. It took me a painful amount of being vulnerable in front of a stranger to figure out that perhaps some kind of _silver lining_ is waiting for me.
