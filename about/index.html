About

Fidelya Fredelina, also known online as fidelyafr (and many other usernames she will NOT disclose) is an Indonesian IT enthusiast whose interest spans multiple domains from data, network, security and recently, wireless engineering. She will touch anything as long as it’s not software engineering.

She obtained her Bachelor’s in Engineering from Universitas Gadjah Mada, majoring in Information Technology. She won’t consider herself a social person but she was quite active during her university life where her involvement peaked at becoming a Vice President of Night Login.

She dreams of leaving the country and pursue a Master’s (possibly also a PhD) degree somewhere in Europe to continue her research in IoT and wireless security. She’s open to any odd IT (and to some extend electronics) job as long as it’s fun and “technically challenging” enough. Have a job opening? PLEASE email her asap.

Not all serious and ambitious, she makes music in her free time. Check that out here. Her favorite artist is STARSET and their third studio album DIVISIONS has a special place in her heart. It makes no sense that she listens to cinematic rock, metal, and indie-rock but produces video game music. Oh well, I guess that’s what happens when you grow up playing UNDERTALE and DELTARUNE.
